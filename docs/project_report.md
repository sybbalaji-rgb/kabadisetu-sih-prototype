# KabadiSetu: Smart India Hackathon 2026 Project Report
## Problem Statement ID: SIH26229
### Category: Smart Automation / Environmental Circular Economy

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**KabadiSetu** is an inclusive digital infrastructure designed to formally integrate India's 4.5+ million informal waste collectors (*kabadiwalas*) into the authorized e-waste and scrap recycling ecosystem. By combining multimodal edge AI vision, cryptographic price-locking (FairLock), cooperative logistics clustering, and digital material passports (DMP), KabadiSetu transforms informal, unorganized scrap gathering into verified, auditable, and remunerative supply chains aligned with CPCB and JNARDDC standards.

---

## 2. Key Challenges Addressed
1. **Financial Deprivation:** Informal collectors receive 30-50% below benchmark commodity prices due to non-transparent middleman cartels.
2. **Occupational Hazards:** Unskilled informal dismantling of hazardous items (Li-ion batteries, PCBs) releases toxic dioxins, lead, and mercury into ambient environments.
3. **EPR Credit Exclusion:** Millions of metric tons of scrap collected informally are excluded from formal Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) credit schemes.
4. **Logistics Friction:** Small batches (<15 kg) are rejected by formal recyclers due to disproportionate freight and collection overhead.

---

## 3. Innovation Highlights
- **Real-Time Gemini AI Scrap Classifier:** Automatically distinguishes intricate electronic scrap (e.g. computer mice, smartphone adapters, motherboards) and provides immediate recovery guidance and handling precautions.
- **FairLock Protocol:** Enforces transparent price commitments with mandatory reason codes if collection weights or conditions vary.
- **Smart Cluster Pickups:** Dynamically aggregates informal micro-sellers within urban wards to achieve container-scale bulk dispatch.
- **Digital Material Passport:** Supplies end-to-end provenance verification, enabling recyclers to claim certified EPR credits while funneling EPR royalties directly back to the original informal collector.
- **Multilingual Inclusive UI:** Full localization across 13 Indian languages with synthetic voice readout and hands-free voice command guidance.

---

## 4. Impact Assessment & Scalability
- **Income Uplift:** Projected **28% to 42% increase in net earnings** for informal waste pickers through direct-to-recycler disintermediation.
- **Environmental Remediation:** Drastic reduction in open-air wire burning and acid leaching through incentivized delivery of unsplit, sorted components.
- **National Scale:** Built on cloud-native serverless foundations capable of scaling to millions of daily micro-transactions across all urban local bodies (ULBs) in India.
